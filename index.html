About

MADRID · ES

VANDYXX is the female DJ duo of Noelia Moya & Essie de Vall — two open-format artists based in Madrid, bending Latin, urban, house and electronic energy into one hypnotic, dancefloor-shaking show.

Noelia Moya

DJ · Open Format · Latin Energy

A magnetic performer with a community of millions across social platforms, Noelia brings high-energy open-format sets packed with Latin, urban and house flavors — the kind of party that doesn't stop once the beat drops.

Essie de Vall

DJ · Open Format · Madrid

From Los Angeles to Madrid, Essie is an open-format DJ and resident at clubs like Opium, Kapital, Ramses and Kube. Nominated Best DJ Spain 20/21, she has shared the stage with Pitbull, Wizkid, Nervo and Jazzy Jeff, and played Madrid Pride before 30,000+ people.
